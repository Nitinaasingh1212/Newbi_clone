03·How it works

From apply to paid.

Four steps. Your dashboard shows where you are on every campaign.

A Professional Instagram account (Creator or Business).

Switching takes a minute in Instagram settings. It lets us confirm your numbers through Instagram itself, with no screenshots.

    Apply

  2. Step 02

04·Questions

Before you apply.

01Is it free to join?

Yes. No application fee, no subscription, no cut taken from what a brief pays you.

02What do I need to apply?

An Instagram account with a real audience and a Professional (Creator or Business) account, so we can confirm your numbers through Instagram itself.

03How big does my following need to be?

Each brief sets its own minimum, and many are open to smaller creators with an engaged, local audience. You will see the number on every campaign.

04What kind of campaigns are there?

Brand campaigns across lifestyle, fashion, food, tech and music, event coverage at shows we run, and campus ambassador programmes.

05How do I submit my work?

From your Newbi dashboard: paste your post links, add screenshots if the brief asks, and track approval there.

06Can I refer other creators?

Yes. You get a personal referral code once you are in.
