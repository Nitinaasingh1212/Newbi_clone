01Who we are

Newbi is run by Newbi Entertainment & Marketing LLP (“Newbi”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers newbi.live, the Newbi app at app.newbi.live, our WhatsApp number and emails, and the creator, ticketing, guestlist and community services we run through them.

We decide why and how your personal data is used, so under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the “data fiduciary” for it. Questions, requests and complaints go to privacy@newbi.live.

02What we collect

We only collect what a feature needs. What that is depends on what you use:

Your account

  • How you sign in: Google, email and password, or your phone number. For phone sign-in and phone verification we send a one-time code, by SMS or on WhatsApp, whichever you choose.
  • Your profile: name, photo, email, phone number (and whether it’s verified), city, college, whether you’re a student, occupation, gender and interests if you choose to share them, and your notification preferences.
  • If two accounts turn out to share a phone number or email, we merge them into one at your request, so one person has one account.

Tickets, passes and guestlists

  • Your name, email and phone number, what you booked, the amount, any coupon, and the Razorpay payment reference for your payment.
  • Your passes and their QR codes, and for some lists the task you submit (a link, a file or a short answer) and how many guests you bring.

Creators

  • Your creator profile: name, contact details, city, niches, rates, whether you do barter, bio, college, and your Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn or X handles.
  • Your public Instagram profile details: username, name, follower and post counts, account type and profile picture. See “Instagram” below.
  • Campaign applications: the details on the form, your consent to WhatsApp updates (with the time and wording you agreed to), the links and screenshots of the posts you deliver, and your status in each campaign.
  • Payouts: the UPI ID or bank account (name on the account, account number, IFSC and bank) you give us to pay you, and the amount, date and reference of each payout. We keep these in our accounts so we can pay you and keep tax records, and we only ask for them when a campaign payout is due.
  • Campaign feedback: after a campaign, your rating, comments and, if you write one, your testimonial, with your answer on whether we may publish it. We only show a testimonial on our website if you said yes, in the way you chose (your name and Instagram handle, or your first name only), and you can change that or ask us to take it down from the same link at any time.

Giveaways, gigs, forms and messages

  • Giveaway entries: your name, contact details, city, college, Instagram handle, your answer, tasks and referral points. The top entries’ names and scores, and the winner’s name, are shown on the giveaway page.
  • Volunteer gig applications, form responses (whatever the form asks, including uploaded files), newsletter sign-ups, and enquiries you send us through the contact form.
  • WhatsApp messages you send to our number, including photos and voice notes. We store the text and keep a reference to media files held by Meta; we don’t keep a copy of the files.

Collected automatically

  • When you open or click a marketing email or one of our short links, we record that it happened, with the time, your IP address and browser details. Ticket, pass and account emails are not tracked.
  • When you sign or view a proposal, agreement or invoice we share with you, we record your IP address as part of the record.
  • Push notification tokens for the devices where you turned on notifications.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.
  • Only if you allow analytics: page views and page load times, counted with Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. They use no cookies, aren’t linked to your account, and we strip codes and references from the page addresses before they’re sent.

We don’t use advertising trackers (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel), and we never sell your personal data.

03How we use it

  • To run your account, sign you in and verify your phone number.
  • To issue and deliver tickets, passes and guestlist entries, confirm payments, and check people in at the door.
  • To review creator applications, match creators to campaigns, run those campaigns with brands, review deliverables and pay creators.
  • To send you messages about what you signed up for: by email, WhatsApp, push notification and in the app. Marketing emails only go out if you haven’t unsubscribed.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse: duplicate accounts, fake payment references, referral manipulation and spam. This includes rate limits on codes and forms.
  • To answer your enquiries and support requests, and to keep records we need for accounting, tax and legal reasons.

We use your data because you gave consent (for example, when you apply as a creator or opt in to WhatsApp updates), because it’s needed to provide something you asked for, or because the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time; see Your choices.

04WhatsApp, email and notifications

We send WhatsApp messages from our business number through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform: sign-in and verification codes when you ask for one on WhatsApp, tickets and passes, order and guestlist updates, and, if you’re a creator who opted in, campaign updates (shortlists, group invites, reminders, deliverable requests, payouts and a feedback request after the campaign). Meta processes these messages under its own terms and policies.

  • Reply STOP (or tap “Stop updates”) to stop campaign updates on WhatsApp. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Ticket, pass and account emails still reach you because they’re part of the service.
  • Push notifications only arrive on devices where you allowed them, and you can turn them off in your browser or phone settings.

05Instagram

Creators can connect Instagram in two ways:

  • Instagram login. You sign in to Instagram and allow the Newbi Live app to read your basic profile (the instagram_business_basic permission): user ID, username, name, follower and post counts, account type and profile picture. We store the access token Instagram gives us on our servers, never in your browser, and refresh it and your follower count once a day while your creator profile is active. We don’t read your messages, comments or posts, and we never post for you.
  • Your handle. When you give us a handle, we look up the public profile through Meta’s Instagram API or, if that fails, from the public profile page or a lookup service (Apify or RapidAPI). We keep the result for up to a day so we don’t look it up again.

We use this only to verify the account is yours and to place you in the right campaign tier. Your profile picture is copied to our image host so it shows on your creator profile. To disconnect, delete your creator profile (see Delete your data) or remove “Newbi Live” in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.

06Who we share it with

We don’t sell your personal data. We share it only in these cases:

  • With brands, when you take part in their campaign: your public creator profile, your posts and how they performed. Your phone number, email, UPI ID and bank details are not shared with brands unless you agree.
  • With event organisers and venues, the details needed to admit you (for example, the name on a pass), when we run entry for them.
  • With the service providers below, who process data for us under contract and only for that purpose.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety or our rights, for example in response to a valid request from authorities.

Service providers

Google Firebase and Google Cloud
Sign-in, database, file storage, push notifications and the reCAPTCHA check on SMS codes.
Vercel
Hosting for our website, app and servers, and, only if you allow analytics, counting page visits and load times.
Meta
WhatsApp messages and codes (WhatsApp Business Platform) and Instagram connection and lookups.
Google Workspace
Sending and receiving our email, and the spreadsheets our team exports to Google Sheets to work on.
Cloudinary
Storing images and files you upload, such as profile photos, post screenshots and payment screenshots.
Razorpay
All payments for tickets and invoices, by UPI, card or netbanking. Razorpay handles your payment details; we receive only whether it was paid and its payment reference.
Apify and RapidAPI
Backup lookups of public Instagram profiles by handle.
Google Gemini and OpenRouter
AI tools our team uses to write content, and translating our public listings into Hinglish. Your account data isn’t sent to them.
YouTube, Vimeo and Instagram
Videos embedded on some event and ConcertZone pages. They load only if you allow embedded videos, and these services may then set their own cookies.

Some of these providers store data outside India. We use providers with recognised security practices, and we stay responsible for how your data is handled.

07Payments

Everything you pay us, for tickets and invoices, is paid in Razorpay’s checkout by UPI, card or netbanking. We never see your bank or card details, only whether the payment went through and its Razorpay reference. Razorpay’s terms and privacy policy apply to the payment. Creator payouts are sent by UPI or bank transfer to the UPI ID or bank account you give us. We never ask for your UPI PIN, a password or an OTP.

08How long we keep it

  • Sign-in and verification codes: only a scrambled (hashed) version is stored, for 10 minutes, and each code works once.
  • WhatsApp messages in our inbox: 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Your account, profile, tickets, passes and applications: for as long as your account is open, then deleted or anonymised as described below.
  • Instagram access tokens: until you delete your creator profile or disconnect; Instagram tokens also expire after 60 days unless refreshed.
  • Payment, ticket and payout records: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, even after your account is deleted.
  • Email open and click records: for as long as we need them to measure our campaigns, and no longer than 24 months.

When we no longer need data, we delete it or remove what links it to you.

09Your rights and choices

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct or update it. Most of your profile you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Delete it, or withdraw consent you gave (for example to WhatsApp updates).
  • Name someone who can exercise these rights for you if you die or can no longer do so.

Write to privacy@newbi.live from the email on your account, or tell us the phone number on it, so we can confirm it’s you. We reply within 7 days and act on requests within 30 days. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10Delete your data

Delete your creator profile (and Instagram connection)

In the Newbi app, open your creator profile, go to the Profile tab and choose Delete creator profile. This removes your creator profile, your Instagram connection and access token straight away. Your Newbi account, tickets and passes stay.

Delete your whole Newbi account

Email privacy@newbi.live with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email on your account (or tell us the phone number on it). We confirm it’s you, then within 30 days delete your account, profile, creator profile, Instagram data, applications, entries and messages.

We keep only what the law requires: records of payments, tickets and payouts for tax and accounting. Those are kept separately and deleted when the required period ends.

Remove Newbi from Instagram

In Instagram, go to Settings → Apps and websites, find Newbi Live and remove it. That stops our access straight away; to delete what we already hold, use one of the options above.

11Cookies and storage on your device

The first time you visit, we ask what you’re happy with. Saying no is as easy as saying yes, and nothing optional loads until you choose.

Essential (always on)

These make the site work, so they don’t need your consent:

  • Keeping you signed in (Firebase stores this in your browser).
  • Your language and theme choice, shared across newbi.live so the site and the app agree.
  • A small “signed in” hint with your first name and photo, so newbi.live can greet you.
  • Your cookie choice itself, so we don’t ask again on every page (kept for 6 months).
  • Offline copies of your passes, so they open at the gate without signal, and drafts of forms you haven’t finished.

Optional (only with your OK)

  • Analytics: page views and load times with Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. No cookies, no ads, not linked to your account.
  • Embedded videos: videos from YouTube, Vimeo and Instagram on some pages. These services set their own cookies; without your OK you see a cover image instead.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a no to everything optional. You can change your choice any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page and on your profile in the app. Push notifications are only turned on when you tap “Turn on” and your browser asks you. We don’t use advertising cookies, and clearing your browser data removes all of the above.

12Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by role: only our team members who need it can see personal data, and Instagram tokens and payment details are kept away from the browser. Codes are stored hashed and expire quickly, and we rate-limit sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

13Age

Newbi is for people aged 18 and over. If you’re under 18, you need a parent or guardian’s consent to use Newbi, and some features (such as creator campaigns and ticketed shows marked 18+) aren’t open to you. If you believe a child has given us data without that consent, write to privacy@newbi.live and we’ll delete it.

14Changes to this policy

When we change how we use data, we update this page and the date at the top. If the change matters, for example a new purpose or a new kind of data, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before it applies.

15Contact

Privacy questions, data requests and complaints: privacy@newbi.live. Our Grievance Officer handles every complaint sent to this address. For anything else, write to support@newbi.live.
