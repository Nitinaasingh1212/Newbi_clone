Privacy Policy

Prototype draft. This describes what the prototype actually stores. Have it reviewed and add OSH Entertainment's contact details for privacy requests before launch.

What we collect

  • Volunteers: name, mobile number, date of birth, preferred city, skill or department, experience and availability, and optionally email, area, gender, languages and an emergency contact.
  • Aadhaar: we check the number's format when you register, then keep only its last 4 digits and a one-way fingerprint used to stop the same number being registered twice. We do not store your full Aadhaar number.
  • Volunteering: the events you send interest for, the team's decisions, and the ratings and comments given after each event.
  • Vendors: company name, contact person, mobile number, city, vendor category, GST number (if given) and the services you describe.
  • Feedback: the category, your message and, only if you give it, your name. Feedback is not linked to your account or phone number.

Why we use it

  • To verify volunteers' identity for the safety of events, attendees and crews.
  • To show volunteers events in their city, handle their interest and contact them about shifts.
  • To calculate points, tiers and city leaderboards.
  • To review and verify vendors and contact them about events.
  • To act on feedback and improve how events are run.

Who can see it

  • The OSH coordination team can see volunteer profiles (with the Aadhaar number masked), interest responses, vendor registrations and feedback.
  • Other volunteers see only your first name and last initial, city, tier, points and average rating on the leaderboard. The home page's Star Volunteers list shows the same details, plus skill, for the top-ranked volunteers.
  • Sign-in codes are sent by SMS through an SMS provider when SMS is switched on.

On your device

The site keeps a few things in your browser's local storage: your sign-in token, your light/dark preference, and the choice lists the forms use. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your choices

You can update your city, skill, experience and availability from your profile. To correct other details or delete your account, contact the OSH team.
