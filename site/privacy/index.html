Privacy Policy

Prototype draft. This describes what the prototype actually stores. Have it reviewed and add OSH Entertainment's contact details for privacy requests before launch.

What we collect

  • Volunteers: name, mobile number, email, date of birth, city and area, area of interest or skills, and optionally gender, languages and an emergency contact.
  • Aadhaar: we check the number's format when you register, then keep only its last 4 digits and a one-way fingerprint used to stop the same number being registered twice. We do not store your full Aadhaar number.
  • Volunteering: the events you apply to, the team's decisions, and the ratings and comments given after each event.
  • Ticket bookings: the attendee's name, email and mobile number, and the ticket booked.
  • Event enquiries: the contact details and event details you send from the Organize page.

Why we use it

  • To verify volunteers' identity for the safety of events, attendees and crews.
  • To show volunteers events in their city, handle applications and contact them about shifts.
  • To calculate points, tiers and city leaderboards.
  • To confirm ticket bookings and check them at entry.
  • To reply to event enquiries.

Who can see it

  • The OSH coordination team can see volunteer profiles (with the Aadhaar number masked), applications, bookings and enquiries.
  • Other volunteers see only your first name and last initial, city, tier, points and average rating on the leaderboard.
  • Sign-in codes are sent by SMS through an SMS provider when SMS is switched on.

On your device

The site keeps a few things in your browser's local storage: your sign-in token, your light/dark preference, events you've saved, and copies of tickets you booked on this device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your choices

You can update your city and contact details from your profile. To correct other details or delete your account, contact the OSH team.
