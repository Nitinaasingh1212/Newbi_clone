What we do

Three parts of every great event

Volunteers & crew

A verified, rated crew in every network city: registration desks, guest relations, backstage, crowd and production.

Trusted vendors

City-wise vendors for sound, stage, decor, catering and more, verified by the OSH team before they're listed as partners.

Event operations

The OSH admin team lists events, picks each crew from the volunteers who are interested, and reviews every shift.

How we work

What we care about

Safety first

Every volunteer's Aadhaar is verified before they're confirmed for a crew, and every shift has a coordinator.

Recognition that's earned

Ratings, points and the Elite Crew ladder are transparent, so good work is visible and rewarded with priority assignments.

Local in every city

Volunteers see and work events in their own city, so crews know the venues and the routes.

We listen

Volunteer feedback goes straight to OSH management, anonymously if you prefer.
