04·How it works

From a brief to a full room.

Four steps, one point of contact. You never chase three vendors for one answer.

  • Step 0101

    Brief

    Tell us the goal, the audience, the city and the budget. A form or a call, whichever is faster.

  • Step 0202

    Plan

    We come back with a plan, a timeline and a quote. You see exactly what happens and when.

  • Step 0303

    Run

    Our crew runs it: artists, production, creators, staffing, entry. You get updates as it happens, not a week later.

  • Step 0404

    Report

    Photos, content links and the numbers that matter for your goal, in one short report.

06·Questions from brands

Before you ask.

01Which cities do you work in?

Across India. Tell us the city or campus and we will tell you how we would run it there.

02How early should we get in touch?

For events and activations, a few weeks ahead gives you the most options. An influencer or digital campaign can go live sooner.

03What does a project cost?

It depends on the format, the city and the scale. Share a rough budget in the form and we plan to it.

04Do you handle permits, production and staffing?

Yes. Venue and campus permits, technical production, fabrication, volunteer crews and running the day are part of the job.

05Can we run a campaign without an event?

Yes. Influencer, digital and social campaigns run on their own or alongside an event or activation.

06How do we see results?

Photos, content links and a short report with the numbers that matter for your goal.
