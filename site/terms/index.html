Using OSH

OSH Entertainment ("OSH") lets you browse and book events, register as a volunteer, and ask OSH to organise an event. By using the site you agree to these terms.

Volunteer accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old to register as a volunteer.
  • You sign in with your own mobile number using a one-time code. Keep the code private.
  • The details you give, including your name, date of birth and Aadhaar number, must be true and your own. One Aadhaar number can be linked to one account.
  • OSH verifies your identity by checking your Aadhaar card before approving you for an event. OSH may reject a verification or suspend an account that gives false details or breaks these terms.

Applying and working events

  • Applying to an event does not guarantee a place. The coordination team approves volunteers based on verification, availability and need.
  • You can withdraw before the event date. If you are approved and don't turn up, you may be marked as a no-show.
  • After each event the coordination team may rate your work. Ratings, completed events and no-shows decide your points, tier and rank, as explained on your profile.
  • Follow the instructions of the event coordinators on the day, including safety instructions.

Tickets and bookings

  • A booking is confirmed when you receive a booking code. Show it at entry.
  • In this prototype, online payment is not connected: paid bookings are confirmed without a charge.
  • Events can change or be cancelled by their organisers. Where that happens, OSH will try to let attendees know.

Acceptable use

Don't misuse the site: no false bookings, no automated sign-ups, no attempts to access other people's accounts or data, and no interference with how the site works.
