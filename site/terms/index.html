Using OSH

OSH Entertainment ("OSH") runs a Pan India event crew network. On this site you can find crew opportunities at events, register as a volunteer or a vendor, and send feedback to OSH management. OSH is not a ticketing platform and does not sell event tickets. By using the site you agree to these terms.

Volunteer accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old to register as a volunteer.
  • You sign in with your own mobile number using a one-time code. Keep the code private.
  • The details you give, including your name, date of birth and Aadhaar number, must be true and your own. One Aadhaar number can be linked to one account.
  • OSH verifies your identity by checking your Aadhaar card before confirming you for a crew. OSH may reject a verification or suspend an account that gives false details or breaks these terms.

Sending interest and working events

  • You can send interest only for events in your registered city. Sending interest does not guarantee a place: the coordination team confirms volunteers based on verification, skills, availability and need.
  • You can withdraw your interest before the event date. If you are approved and don't turn up, you may be marked as a no-show.
  • After each event the coordination team may rate your work from 1 to 5 stars. Ratings, completed events and no-shows decide your points, your tier (New Joiner, Trained, Experienced, 5-Star Elite) and your rank, as explained on your profile.
  • Follow the instructions of the event coordinators on the day, including safety instructions.

Vendors

  • Vendor registrations are reviewed by OSH. A vendor appears as a verified partner only after OSH has checked their details.
  • The details you give, including any GST number, must be accurate and belong to your business.
  • Registering does not guarantee work. Each engagement is agreed separately with the OSH team.

Feedback

Feedback goes to OSH management. Don't include other people's personal details unless you need them to explain an issue. For anything urgent or unsafe at an event, tell your on-site coordinator straight away.

Acceptable use

Don't misuse the site: no false registrations or feedback, no automated sign-ups, no attempts to access other people's accounts or data, and no interference with how the site works.
